# Progression Validation Rules
1. Center levels must be exactly 1..10.
2. Resident caps must be monotonic and end at 50.
3. Village-development requirements must be monotonic.
4. Center unlock references must resolve to known systems/buildings.
5. Building current-level cap must never exceed Rescue Center level.
6. Job-mastery requirements must reference valid 0..100 thresholds.
7. Bond and resident relationships must never be mandatory center gates in v1.
8. Gold costs must live in balance data rather than core progression code.
9. Final collection must remain achievable without advertisements.
10. Festival must not require every building to be max level.
11. Development score must not double-count resident count or catalog count.
12. Any tuning of development awards must keep all center thresholds mathematically reachable before their respective stages.
