# 50-Cat Roster Balance Review — v1.0 Working Review

## Locked Principles
- 50 catalog cats = 50 unique resident instances.
- No duplicates, rarity tiers or power tiers.
- Discovery difficulty does not imply strength.
- Base abilities express personality; work experience can reshape residents.
- Late cats do not invalidate early cats.
- CAT_050 is narratively special, not mechanically superior.

## Ability Review
Initial ability values are generally kept in the 2–8 band. Characters intentionally have shaped profiles rather than flat 5/5/5/5/5 distributions.

Before production lock, run a spreadsheet/script audit for:
- average and min/max of each ability
- total stat distribution
- count of cats whose strongest stat is each ability
- duplicate/near-duplicate stat profiles
- personality-to-stat over-correlation
- whether any cat is an obvious strict upgrade of another

## Personality Review
Eight personality types should be represented across the roster without requiring perfect equality. Personality must not become a disguised rarity or job lock.

## Appearance Review
Before art production:
- thumbnail all 50 at village scale
- flag near-duplicate coat/face silhouettes
- ensure late cats are not automatically more ornate
- preserve readable differences using face marks, fur length, tail, socks, eyes and body silhouette

## Discovery Review
Discovery conditions should cover:
- center progression
- building state
- village development
- game-time/environment
- resident activity
- reusable rescue mechanics

No cat should require an opaque external-guide-only condition. At high collection completion, detailed hints become more explicit.

## Weather Dependency
Weather is not yet a locked v1 system. CAT_044 has a documented fallback condition so roster completion never depends on an unapproved feature.

## Narrative Arc
Early: player actively searches and rescues.
Middle: the growing village itself attracts/uncovers residents.
Late: residents and completed facilities participate in discoveries.
Final: CAT_050 voluntarily arrives at the completed Rescue Town.

This arc should be preserved even if individual names or appearances change during content polish.
